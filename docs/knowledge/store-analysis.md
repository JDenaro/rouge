## Size Guide

### How to Measure
Use a flexible measuring tape with minimal clothing. Four key measurements:
1. **Contorno bajo busto** (under-bust circumference)
2. **Contorno de busto** (full bust circumference)
3. **Contorno de cintura** (waist circumference)
4. **Contorno de cadera** (hip circumference)

**Key advice:** Under-bust should fit firmly but comfortably ("firme pero cómodo"). When between sizes, choose the larger one for enhanced comfort. Bust should be well-supported without spillage or looseness.

### Bra Sizing Chart
Argentine bra sizes correlate under-bust (cm) and full bust (cm). Range: 85B to 140G.
- Under-bust measurements: 83–142 cm
- Full bust measurements: 98–155 cm
- Each size spans a 4-5 cm range for both measurements
- Custom ("a medida") orders available beyond standard range

## Return & Exchange Policy

### Production
- All items are **made-to-order** — no stock held
- **Production time:** ~15–20 business days from order confirmation

### Returns
- "No se aceptan devoluciones por arrepentimiento" — buyer's remorse returns are NOT accepted once production starts
- **Before production begins:** 45% deduction applied (materials and labor already used)
- **Manufacturing impossibility:** 100% refund if the company cannot produce the item

### Exchanges
- Valid ONLY for **proven manufacturing defects**
- The following do NOT qualify for exchange:
  - Minor seam issues or wear
  - Size discrepancies (buyer is responsible for consulting size guide)
  - Change of mind

## Key Business Logic for Next.js Rebuild

1. **Dual pricing:** Every product needs a `regularPrice` and a `transferPrice` (= regular × 0.90 for standard products). Show both clearly.
2. **Installments:** Always show `regularPrice ÷ 3` as "3 cuotas sin interés".
3. **Made-to-order:** Every garment needs a clear "made to order / a pedido" label. No cart-to-stock logic.
4. **Size range:** Standard sizes are 85–130 (bra sizing), many products go to 140+. Custom sizing is offered via WhatsApp for most items.
5. **Color swatches:** Products typically have 2–11 color options. Colors should be selectable on product cards and PDP.
6. **Lead time:** Show 15–20 business day lead time prominently.
7. **WhatsApp CTA:** Primary conversion action. Phone: `+541158861214`.
8. **Shipping:** All products show free shipping. Carriers: Andreani, Correo Argentino, moto, pickup in Morón.
9. **Bundles / Kits:** Some products are multi-item bundles with explicit bundle discount (10% or 30% on top of transfer).
